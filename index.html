How it works

1

Your phone rings first

Nothing changes when you can answer. JobCatch only picks up the calls you miss.

2

It answers as your business

Your business name, your services, your area, your call-out fee. The caller gets looked after instead of a voicemail beep.

3

You get the job

Name, mobile, address, the problem and how urgent it is, ready for you to call back between jobs.

What it handles

  • Answers 24/7, including after hours and weekends
  • Asks the right questions, one at a time, and reads the number back to get it right
  • Gives safe first steps for emergencies, like turning off the water at the mains
  • Answers common questions: service area, hours, call-out fee
  • Never makes up prices or promises. If it doesn't know, it says you'll call back
  • Tells callers honestly that it's a virtual assistant if they ask
